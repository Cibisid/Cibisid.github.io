How recording works

Your band sends heart rate and beat-to-beat (RR) intervals over standard Bluetooth. While this page is open and the band is in range, every minute is saved. From those minutes the page works out your sleep window, overnight HRV and resting heart rate, and the day's strain, and they feed Today, Trends and Sleep.

  • Overnight is what matters. Recovery needs the band connected while you sleep. Leave this tab open, laptop plugged in and near your bed.
  • Stop the laptop sleeping: Windows Settings → System → Power → Screen and sleep → "When plugged in, put my device to sleep after" → Never. The screen can turn off.
  • One connection at a time: close the WHOOP app on your phone or turn the phone's Bluetooth off, or the band stays busy.
  • Daytime strain only counts minutes when you're in range of the laptop. Gaps are shown as gaps, not guessed.
  • No movement sensor is read, so sleep stages aren't available from the band. Sleep timing comes from your heart rate settling.

How the scores are calculated

WHOOP's exact formulas are private. These are open, research-based stand-ins that track the same signals, so treat them as close cousins rather than identical numbers. If an import already includes WHOOP's own recovery or strain, the page shows those instead.

Recovery (1–99%)

Each morning is compared with your own last 30 days. HRV is log-transformed (ln RMSSD) and turned into a z-score; resting HR, respiratory rate and sleep performance get z-scores too. They're weighted 60% HRV · 25% resting HR · 10% sleep · 5% respiratory rate and passed through a logistic curve. Green is 67 or more, yellow 34–66, red 33 or less. The first 4 days are marked "calibrating" because there's no baseline yet.

Strain (0–21)

Cardiovascular load is Banister TRIMP: minutes × heart-rate reserve × 0.64·e1.92·HRR, using max HR from the Tanaka formula (208 − 0.7 × age) unless you set your own. Activity outside workouts is estimated from active calories or steps. Load maps onto a curve that flattens near the top, 21 × (1 − e−TRIMP/128), so each extra point of strain is harder to earn, which is how WHOOP's scale behaves.

Strain target

Green recovery: 14–18. Yellow: 10–14. Red: under 10 (active recovery).

Sleep need & performance

Need = your baseline (7.75 h by default) + 0.08 h for every strain point above 10 the day before + half of the debt you're carrying. Performance = hours asleep ÷ need, capped at 100%. Consistency comes from how much your bed and wake times varied over the last 4 nights.

Training load

Average strain over 7 days ÷ average over 28 days. 0.8–1.3 is the usual productive range; above 1.5 is a sharp spike.

Where data comes from

A WHOOP band with no membership doesn't sync to WHOOP's servers, so its data has to come off the band over Bluetooth. Open-source tools like OpenWhoop (in a Web Bluetooth browser) do that and can export JSON, which this page reads. An Apple Watch works too: ask Claude in chat to pull your Health data into this page.

Not a medical device. Unusual readings, like a resting HR jump or a sudden HRV crash with symptoms, are a reason to talk to a doctor.
