01 · FLAGSHIPShipped

Gauntlet

A safety check that stops a bad AI update before customers see it.

Companies keep updating the AI behind their chatbots and assistants. Sometimes an update quietly gives worse answers, gets slower, or costs more, and customers notice before the team does.

How it works

  1. Tests the new AI version on a fixed set of questions and scores accuracy, made-up answers, speed and cost.
  2. If any score misses its target, the update is blocked.
  3. If it passes, it goes to 5% of users first, then 25%, 50% and everyone. If errors spike, it switches everyone back to the old version automatically.

In short: a driving test plus a probation period for AI models.

02 · AI INFRASTRUCTUREShipped

LLM Gateway

One connection to many AI providers, so an app keeps working when one goes down.

Most apps connect to a single AI provider. When that provider has an outage or slows down, the app breaks for every user. The gateway sits in the middle and can talk to several providers.

How it works

  1. The app sends every AI request to the gateway, in the same format it already uses, so nothing needs rewriting.
  2. The gateway picks a provider based on what the app asked for: the smartest, the fastest, or the cheapest.
  3. If that provider is down, it automatically tries the next one, so the user still gets an answer. It also gives AI agents a shared set of 11 tools and lets one agent hand work to another.

In short: like a phone that switches to another cell network when yours drops.

03 · HEALTHCARE AIShipped

TrialBridge

Finds clinical trials a patient may qualify for, and never guesses.

Clinical trials test new treatments, and each one has a long list of rules about age, diagnosis, lab results and more. Checking one patient against thousands of trials by hand takes hours.

How it works

  1. Searches 2,344 real trials from ClinicalTrials.gov for ones related to the patient's condition.
  2. An AI assistant reads the patient's record and checks each trial's rules one by one.
  3. Every rule comes back as met, not met, or UNKNOWN when the record is missing that information. The assistant is not allowed to guess.

In short: in healthcare, "we need one more lab result" is far safer than a confident wrong answer.

04 · AUTOMATIONShipped

job-watcher

An automatic job hunter that checks hundreds of company career pages for you.

New jobs are posted on hundreds of separate company websites, and the big job boards are cluttered with staffing agencies and repeat listings. Keeping up by hand means refreshing tabs all day.

How it works

  1. Every 3 hours, it visits about 430 company job boards and collects every new posting.
  2. It throws out staffing agencies and roles that won't sponsor.
  3. It scores each remaining job from 1 to 5 for how well it fits, and shows the best ones on a dashboard.

In short: it does the daily job-board refreshing, then hands you a shortlist.

05 · DATA SCIENCERunner-up · AI for Good 2025

Housing Affordability

How much do you need to earn to afford a home? Our team built models to answer it.

Home prices and interest rates keep rising, but most families don't know what income a house really takes. For the State of Florida's AI for Good Hackathon, our team built machine learning models on this question.

How it works

  1. Takes a home's price, down payment, interest rate, taxes and insurance.
  2. Works out the true monthly payment.
  3. Calculates the yearly income needed, and estimates how many households can afford it.

In short: turns "can I afford this house?" into a clear number. We placed runner-up statewide.
